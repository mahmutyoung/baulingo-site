# DTZ phrase frames — reusable phrases

Updated: 1 November 2026.

**120 reusable phrase entries**, organised by situation. Every entry has an A2/B1 level, a German phrase or expandable frame, a Turkish meaning and a complete German example.

The first ten situation groups contain the website’s current **80 phrases**. The final five groups add **40 document-only entries** for housing, school, money, social contact and explanations. These additions have not been added to the website.

Levels are editorial teaching labels for the phrase **together with its example**, not an official assessment. A2 marks basic everyday use; B1 marks more demanding vocabulary, formal interaction or connected expression. The same phrase can appear in simpler or more complex sentences.

An ellipsis indicates material the learner supplies. Reflexive forms such as `sich für … interessieren` are shown in dictionary form; the example demonstrates their use. These are reusable expressions and frames, rather than arbitrary consecutive word fragments.

## Editorial basis

The website entries mirror `src/dtz-study.js` at preparation time. The additional entries are authored extensions of the everyday-life and communicative functions in the Obsidian sentence tables, with newly written examples. They are not claimed as verbatim extracted phrases.

The former 702-row contextual chunk inventory has been removed from this document. Combinations such as `ich Ihnen` or `mit freundlichen` are not counted as reusable phrase entries. The original frequency and ladder tables remain in the Obsidian source folder.

The 120 entries are a teaching list; they do not establish the total number of reusable phrases in that source corpus.
