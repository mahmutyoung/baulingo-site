## Scope and use

All **130 frame families** are included below. Each family has its original reusable template, two seed realizations and all ten ladder variants: five stages for each seed. That preserves every source row instead of collapsing the bank into one example per family.

Braces mark template slots. Supply a grammatically compatible phrase, including the required inflection. For example, in `{service} fährt um {time} ab.`, `{service}` can be `Der Bus` and `{time}` can be `acht Uhr`. The article and singular verb must still agree. In `Wegen {reason}`, check the noun phrase’s case; arbitrary word substitution is not sufficient.

A ladder is a family of related communicative realizations, not a guarantee that every row can be produced by substituting slots into the same literal template. A relative clause, modal construction or paraphrase can change its syntax. Use the actual variant as the answer key when building an exercise.

The five labels A2.1, A2.2, A2–B1, B1.1 and B1.2 are progression bands from the source, not independently assessed exam levels. Greetings and closings are kept as formula frames; they are not relabelled as complete sentences.
